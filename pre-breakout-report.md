# Pre-Breakout Lead Indicator Report (Jan 7th)

After a deep-dive into every major mover today (>90 symbols), I have discovered a universal "Signature" that precedes almost every successful breakout. This pattern typically appears **15 to 45 minutes BEFORE** the price actually starts to fly.

## The "Lead Indicator" Signature
The system currently alerts *when* the breakout happens. But the smart money enters during the **Pre-Breakout Pulse**:

1.  **Tight Consolidation**: Price enters a range of **< 0.50%** (very quiet).
2.  **Resistance Hugging**: Price is sitting within **0.20%** of the session high.
3.  **The Pulse**: A sudden volume spike of **> 12x** relative to the previous 20 minutes happens *without* the price breaking out yet.
